Achint Sharma

I am a graduate student in Cognitive Science interested in multisensory integration, the role of working memory in audiovisual integration, and temporal dynamics of audiovisual speech perception. My research examines how the brain combines auditory and visual information, and how cognitive processes shape audiovisual speech perception.

I use behavioral experiments, EEG, and data-analysis tools to study perception and cognition. My broader goal is to understand how people maintain and integrate information across sensory modalities over time.

About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Cognitive Science with training in experimental design, behavioral data collection, EEG data collection and analysis, and management of large research datasets. My work is broadly situated at the intersection of perception, cognition, and cognitive neuroscience.

I am especially interested in how auditory and visual signals are integrated during speech perception, and how working memory contributes to maintaining audiovisual information.

Research Interests

  • Multisensory Integration: How the brain combines information from different sensory modalities, especially auditory and visual signals.
  • Audiovisual Speech Perception: How visual speech cues influence what we hear and how speech perception changes when auditory and visual timing is altered.
  • Working Memory: How people maintain and integrate audiovisual information over short periods of time.
  • EEG and Neural Timing: How neural activity reflects sensory processing, temporal alignment, and multisensory integration.
