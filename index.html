I am a graduate student interested in multisensory perception, EEG and cognitive neuroscience.

About Me

I am interested in understanding how the brain integrates information across sensory systems, with a particular focus on multisensory integration and the role of working memory in shaping perception. My work involves designing behavioral and EEG-based experiments, collecting and analyzing behavioral and neural data, and managing large research datasets. Through my research, I aim to explore how working memory and sensory processing interact to support perception and cognition. .

Research Interests: Multisensory processing, neural basis of multisensory integration and role of working memory in perception and integration of sensory information

  • EEG and neural oscillations
  • Audio-visual perception
  • Experimental psychology
