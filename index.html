Achint Sharma

I am a graduate student interested in multisensory perception, its neural basis, and the role of working memory in multisensory integration.

About Me

My research focuses on how the brain integrates information across sensory systems, especially audiovisual integration in speech and the role of working memory in shaping perception. I have experience designing behavioral and EEG-based experiments, collecting and analyzing behavioral and neural data, and managing large research datasets. Through my research, I aim to explore how working memory and sensory processing interact to support perception and cognition. .

Research Interests:

Multisensory processing, neural basis of audiovisual integration in speech and role of working memory in perception and integration of sensory information.

  • EEG and neural oscillations
  • Audio-visual perception
  • Experimental design
